# Fenerbahçe Match Calendars

[Türkçe](README.md) · **English**

Match schedules of Fenerbahçe's men's and women's first teams in football, basketball and volleyball, published
as subscribable calendars that update themselves.

You subscribe once. Newly announced matches, date and time changes, and results appear in the calendar
automatically. Kick-off times are shown in the time zone of the device viewing the calendar.

> A volunteer project made by fans. It is not affiliated with Fenerbahçe Spor Kulübü or any federation.

Each team is published as a separate calendar, so you can subscribe only to the teams you follow. For every match
in one calendar, choose **All Teams**; in that case do not also subscribe to individual teams, or matches will
appear twice. A reminder before each match can be chosen on the website.

## How matches appear

Calendar entries are in Turkish:

```
⚽ E · Fenerbahçe – Galatasaray
🏐 K · Fenerbahçe 3–1 VakıfBank
🏀 E · Anadolu Efes – Fenerbahçe (saat belli değil)
```

- **E / K:** men's (Erkek) or women's (Kadın) team.
- **(saat belli değil):** the day is set but the time is not announced yet; the same event is updated once it is.
- **ERTELENDİ / İPTAL:** postponed or cancelled.

Each event lists the competition, round, venue, kick-off in Turkish time and a link to the official match page.
Matches are added once their date has been announced by the relevant federation or organiser.

## Competitions covered

- **Men's Football:** Süper Lig, Turkish Cup, Turkish Super Cup and UEFA competitions
- **Men's Basketball:** Basketbol Süper Ligi, Presidential Cup, Turkish Cup and EuroLeague
- **Women's Volleyball:** Sultanlar Ligi and Turkish Cup; CEV Champions League once CEV publishes its schedule
- **Men's Volleyball:** Efeler Ligi and Turkish Cup; CEV Cup once CEV publishes its schedule
- **Women's Basketball:** Kadınlar Basketbol Süper Ligi, Presidential Cup, Turkish Cup and EuroLeague Women
- **Women's Football:** Kadın Futbol Süper Ligi and UEFA women's competitions

Cup matches are added as pairings are confirmed. Friendlies are not published by the federations and are not
included.
